Detailed Description

Applies battery qualification, wake-lock accounting, and sleep eligibility. Product-level policy is kept here while IPowerManagement owns clocks, GPIO wake sources, and chip sleep APIs. This separation makes thresholds and activity decisions host-testable and keeps app code from bypassing radio coordination in the platform layer.
