Detailed Description

Coordinates Wi-Fi availability with independent service clients.

Each NetworkHandle is a scoped radio-use lease. The manager keeps the radio active while at least one lease exists and powers it down after the final lease and idle policy permit. This makes resource lifetime explicit and avoids one app disconnecting Wi-Fi while another service still needs it.
